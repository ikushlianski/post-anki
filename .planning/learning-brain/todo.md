## Notes

- A note can attach to a topic, gap, or source, matching how similar features work.
- Notes stay searchable immediately after being created or edited.
- Never push notes at the learner through daily nudges; review stays pull-only. Surfacing them would recreate the backlog guilt the product already rejected, so treat any proposal to change this as a decision for a human.
- A highlight and a note are the same kind of item, just flagged differently; don't split them apart later without revisiting that choice.
- Never show a count or badge of notes waiting to be reviewed anywhere in the product; that would recreate the backlog pressure this feature deliberately avoids.
