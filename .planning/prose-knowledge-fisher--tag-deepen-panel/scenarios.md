# Scenarios — tag-deepen-panel

- [ ] A tag with status "struggling" or "practicing" appears in the panel, linked to
  /knowledge/answer scoped to that tag.
- [ ] A tag with status "mastered" or "new" does NOT appear in the panel.
- [ ] No struggling/practicing tags at all shows a quiet empty-state message, not an error or a
  blank section.
- [ ] No accept/reject control, no count badge, appears anywhere in or near this panel.
