# Report: Show today's reviewed cards and subjects

## What changed (plain words)
A new read-only page, /knowledge/reviewed-today, lists every card the learner graded today, grouped by subject, with how many times each was graded and the last grade. "Today" is the learner's local day (the browser time zone is sent along), so a grade at 23:50 and one at 00:10 land on different days. Undone grades never show, each card appears once under its own subject (no roll-up to parent subjects), and cards with no subject go under "Unsorted". The done-for-today screen and the dashboard both link to it. No schema change, no migration, no new package.

## Defaults and deviations
- Endpoint GET /knowledge-bits/reviews/today?tz=, capped at 500 cards with a truncated flag. When truncated, totalCards/totalReviews count the shown (newest 500) cards only.
- The done-screen link shows only in the "Done for today" state, not the "no approved cards yet" state.
- The dashboard link sits above the Unsorted/subject list inside the non-search view.
- No lint command exists for apps/api or apps/web (only apps/bot has one), so lint was not run.
