# Scenarios — fill-blank retry-and-claim concurrency (Unit B)

- [ ] Two scans racing to claim the same unclaimed bit: only one claim succeeds, the loser skips it with no LLM call
- [ ] A bit with a fresh (non-stale) claim from another in-flight scan is not selected as a candidate
- [ ] A bit with a stale claim (older than the staleness window) is selected as a candidate again
- [ ] A bit that failed once, under the attempts cap, is still a scan candidate and gets retried
- [ ] A bit that has hit the attempts cap is excluded from future scan candidates
- [ ] A successful generation clears the claim and leaves attempts/failedAt alone
- [ ] A failed generation increments attempts and sets failedAt, without touching a bit generated by a concurrent winner
