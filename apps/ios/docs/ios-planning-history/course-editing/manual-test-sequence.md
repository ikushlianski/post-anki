## Environment
- Local post-anki API with a token in Settings; simulator iOS 17+. Use a throwaway course.

## Steps
1. Open a course. Expect "Edit course structure" after the stats section.
2. Open it, tap +, add module "Basics". Expect it listed. In its section tap "Add topic" twice.
3. Use the module menu (ellipsis) or long-press a topic, Rename. Expect the new title.
4. Tap Edit and drag a topic. Done, pull to refresh. Expect the order to stick.
5. With two or more modules tap "Order modules", drag, Done. Expect the order to stick.
6. Comment on a topic and a module. Expect "Comment saved."
7. Delete a topic. Expect a confirm about study history. Delete a module. Expect a confirm with its topic count.
8. Go back. Expect the course screen to show every change.
9. Empty course: expect "No modules yet".
